Two Binding Mechanisms
Same result — two ways your data can be shaped
A restaurant menu page: outer repeater shows Categories, inner repeater shows Dishes per category. How you connect them depends on where the relationship lives in your schema.
Mechanism 1
Array field on the parent
The Menu Categories collection has a dishes [] array field that stores the dishes directly. The inner repeater binds to that field — one context, no extra setup.

Mechanism 2
Separate collection with a back-reference
The Dishes collection has a category reference field pointing back to Menu Categories. A second context is added — the platform automatically filters it to the current category row.
